Multimodal and Multichannel Human–Computer Interaction

Project overview

This project explores an intelligent interaction framework that integrates multimodal and multichannel information for representative tasks. It investigates unobtrusive interaction services and shared collaborative spaces through user intent sensing and inference.

My contribution

Oct 2020 — Aug 2023Research contributor

Intent-Aware Interaction Strategies and Human Factors Evaluation

Project overview

Combining ergonomics and intent recognition, this project investigates how users understand information and control interactions in complex information systems. It explores human–machine interaction strategies that support visual perception, decision-making, and action.

My contribution

I was responsible for the work package on multichannel human–machine interaction strategies and ergonomic evaluation. I designed and conducted validation experiments, and contributed to task planning, progress reports, and research reports.
