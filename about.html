A little more
about me.

I’m an optimistic and friendly person. Beyond research, I enjoy reading science fiction, listening to music across genres, exploring new places, and making things by hand.

I like to imagine the world as an open-world game: attention renders the details I focus on more clearly, while action leads me to explore new landscapes. Travel, reading, making, and research are ways I stay curious and experience the world firsthand; observation and reflection continually reshape my understanding of it.
