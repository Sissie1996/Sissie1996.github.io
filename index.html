01 / RESEARCH

From human behavior
to responsive systems.

My work connects human factors, machine learning, and interaction design to make user interfaces more intuitive.

Research interests

The questions and contexts that connect my work.

Natural and multimodal interaction

Exploring how natural behavior and multiple input modalities can support intuitive interaction between people and systems.

Attention and intent understanding

Understanding attention, intentions, and goals through behavioral cues, and modeling how they unfold during interaction.

Adaptive interfaces and proactive feedback

Investigating whether, when, and how interfaces should respond to users’ needs through adaptive visual feedback.

Multi-user and multi-device interaction

Designing interaction techniques and interface organization that support coordination across people, tasks, and devices.

Research approach

Observing behavior. Reading intent. Shaping interaction.

a

Observing behavior

PERCEPTION AND ACTION

I examine how natural behavior relates to internal cognitive states such as attention and intention. Through behavioral experiments and multimodal data analysis, I explore how users observe targets, plan actions, and complete representative interaction tasks.

FOCUS

Cognitive mechanisms and behavioral patterns

b

Reading intent

BEHAVIORAL CUES → INTENT UNDERSTANDING

I translate natural behavioral cues into computational representations of user states, exploring how to recognize and predict attentional states, interaction intentions, and intended targets. I evaluate model performance across users and task contexts to help systems understand what users are doing and what they aim to achieve.

FOCUS

Predictive performance and generalization

c

Shaping interaction

INTENT UNDERSTANDING → PROACTIVE FEEDBACK

Building on an understanding of user behavior and intent, I study whether, when, and how interfaces should offer assistance. Through the design and evaluation of proactive visual feedback, I explore how interfaces can support perception and action while improving interaction efficiency and user experience.

FOCUS

User benefit and human–machine collaboration
