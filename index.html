03 / ABOUT

A researcher’s curiosity.
A designer’s perspective.

I work at the intersection of how people think and how systems respond.

I hold a PhD in Mechanical Engineering (Industrial Design) from Southeast University, where I worked with Prof. Chengqi Xue. My doctoral research explored intent perception and interface feedback through natural eye–hand behavior in virtual reality.

During my doctoral studies, I also conducted research with Prof. Jeremy R. Cooperstock at McGill University’s Shared Reality Lab, focusing on user intent recognition and prediction.

My experience spans experimental design, multimodal sensing, behavioral analysis, and interactive prototyping—connecting research questions with tangible interface design.
