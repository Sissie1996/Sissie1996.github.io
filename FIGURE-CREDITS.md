# Paper figure sources

Figures are from Xiaoxi Du's first-author publications and were individually selected by the author for this homepage. Original image composition and colors are preserved. Lossless thumbnails are resized for display; the image viewer links to the full-resolution originals and source papers. The English descriptions on the homepage summarize the figure content rather than reproducing the original captions.
