# Paper figure sources

Figures are from Xiaoxi Du's publications and were individually selected by her for this homepage. Scientific content, annotations, and colors are preserved. The two keyboard/bare-hand panels are arranged side by side in a separate composite; the original vertical figure is retained. Lossless thumbnails are resized for display; the image viewer links to the full-resolution originals and source papers. The English descriptions on the homepage summarize the figure content rather than reproducing the original captions.
